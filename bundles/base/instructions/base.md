## General conventions

- This project's skills and instructions come from `setup` bundles listed in `setup.yaml`. Use the `setup-workbench` skill to add, remove or update them.
- Do not edit generated skill folders or text inside `setup:begin` markers. Put project rules outside the markers.
- State what you verified and how. Label anything you did not check as a guess.
- Ask before you take an irreversible action, such as deleting data, force-pushing or publishing.
